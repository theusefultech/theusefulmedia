1 iPhone app that warns you before every subscription renews

A private list of everything that bills you, with a reminder before each charge.

THE USEFUL TECH

🔍 THE USEFUL FIND · MONDAY DISCOVERY · ISSUE #018

👋 HEY, IT'S RAJA

Black Friday is 27 November. It's the week a lot of us start free trials and grab yearly plans at a discount, and most of them renew on their own a year later at full price.

This week's find is a small iPhone app that keeps every one of those dates in 1 list and taps you on the shoulder before each charge.

🔍 The find: Rebills

Rebills is a subscription tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac from Kojya, a tiny independent developer. Version 2.0 rebuilt the whole app this year with a new design, new widgets and Siri support.

What it does

You add what you pay for: streaming, software, the gym, a loan payment, the lawn service, even a kid's allowance. Set how often it repeats and when you want a reminder.

Rebills sorts everything by date, so the top of the list is always the next thing that's going to charge you.

Reminders work for any repeat, from every month to once every 5 years. That covers the yearly plans that hurt most, the ones you signed up for 11 months ago and forgot.

Why it's different

Most subscription trackers want you to log in to your bank so they can find the charges for you. Rebills doesn't, on purpose. No bank or card linking, no account, no analytics and no ads.

Your list syncs privately through iCloud, and you can export all of it as a file whenever you like.

It also doesn't charge you a subscription to track your subscriptions. You pay once, and Family Sharing covers up to 6 people.

The bits I'd use most

  • A Lock Screen widget that shows your next renewal at a glance.
  • A Home Screen widget with a Mark Paid button, so you never open the app for a bill you already paid.
  • Siri. Ask "What rebills do I have?" and it reads you the list.
  • Tracking by card, with the type and last 4 digits, handy when a card expires and you need to know what's on it.
  • 100+ currencies, for anything you pay for abroad.

Tip: before you add anything, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. That's every App Store subscription you have. Add those first, then search your email for the ones billed directly.

📍 Where: App Store, 1 purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac

💻 Works on: iOS 26.4 or later

🎁 Try it: You pay once. No subscription.
