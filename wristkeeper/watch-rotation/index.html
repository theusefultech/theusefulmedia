Watch rotation

A watch rotation is a simple rule for which watch you wear each day, so none of them sit in the box for months. The easiest rule: wear the watch you've ignored longest, unless the weather or the day rules it out.

By Raja, maker of Wristkeeper. Updated 17 November 2026.

Pick 1 rotation rule

I kept wearing the same 3 watches because the choice happened at 7 in the morning, in a hurry. A rule makes the choice for you. Pick 1 and keep it for a month.

  1. Longest unworn first. Each morning, wear the watch that has waited longest. Skip it only if it clashes with the day: a leather strap in the rain, a dress watch at the gym.
  2. Weekly slots. Give each weekday a role, such as a tool watch on Monday, something on a bracelet midweek and the dress piece on Friday, and pick a different watch for each slot every week.
  3. Theme days. Pick by what you're doing, not by the day: Work, Dinner, Gym or Travel. Inside each theme, the watch worn least goes first.

Keep a wear log

A rule only works if you know what you wore. Write down the date and the watch every day you wear one, on paper or in a spreadsheet. After a month, the log shows which watches are in the rotation and which ones only feel like they are.

The same log gives you each watch's cost per wear: what you paid divided by the days you wore it. A high number usually marks a watch that has waited too long in the box.

Set it out the night before

  • Put tomorrow's watch at the front of the box, so the choice is already made when you get dressed.
  • If it's an automatic that has stopped, wind it and set the time the night before, not on your way out, and set the date as its manual allows.
  • Check tomorrow's weather and plans once. A diver on a bracelet for the rain, the dress watch for the dinner.

Questions

How many watches should be in a rotation?

As many as you will wear. Count how many of your watches you wore in the last 90 days: those are your rotation, and the rest are the ones a rule like longest unworn first brings back.

Should I rotate automatic watches?

Wearing them in turn is the point of owning several. An automatic that sat in the box for a few days will often have stopped, so wind it and set the time before you put it on.
