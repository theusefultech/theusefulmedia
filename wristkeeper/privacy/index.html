Published by Rajasekaran Ponnuchamy. Wristkeeper is a watch collection and wear log that lives on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Wristkeeper has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third party services, and no server of its own. Your collection, photos, wear history, and settings stay on your device.

Weather

If you allow location access, the app uses your location only to request local weather from Apple's weather service (Apple Weather) and does not send it anywhere else. That request is handled by Apple's system frameworks, and Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. The last weather reading is kept only on your device, and it contains no location. If you deny location access, the app simply shows no weather.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about them leaves it.

Retention and deletion

Everything is stored on your device until you delete it. Deleting a watch deletes its photos and its wear history. Deleting the app deletes all app data.

Changes

If any of the above ever changes, this policy will be updated before the app behaves differently.
