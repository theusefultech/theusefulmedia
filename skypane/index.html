Questions. Short answers.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Will it slow my Mac down?

On an M4 Mac mini with a 5K display it uses 3.7% CPU while snowing and about 285 MB of memory.

Do my clicks still work?

Yes. Every click goes through the snow to the window or icon under it.

Does it work with 2 displays?

Yes. It works on every display, every Space and in Stage Manager.

Can I pause it?

Click the Skypane icon in the menu bar. Pause, change the weather or turn Real Sky off from there.

What does it need access to?

Nothing for the weather and effects. It only needs to know where your windows are, and macOS shares that without a permission prompt.

Yes. The free download has snow and rain behind your windows and a tour of every effect. A key unlocks the 3 worlds and the rest.
