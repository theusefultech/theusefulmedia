Questions

Who counts as a person on the plan?

Anyone at your company who uses the kit. Up to 5, 15 or 50 people depending on the plan. Share the files inside your company only.

How do 15 people get the files when 1 person pays?

The person who pays gets 1 download link with every file. Share the files on your company drive or in a shared Claude Project, and everyone on the plan uses them from there.

What arrives right after we pay?

An email with the 4 guides, the Team Rollout Plan and The Friday Client Update as PDFs. Updates arrive the same way while the plan is active.

Can the invoice show our company name and VAT ID?

Yes. Email raja@theusefultech.com with the legal name, address and VAT ID, and the PayPal invoice carries them.

Can we pay by invoice?

Yes. Email raja@theusefultech.com with your company name and team size, and you get a PayPal invoice your finance team can pay by card or PayPal.

What happens when Claude changes?

The guides get updated as Claude adds features. Your team gets each update for as long as the plan is active.

Do we need Claude Team?

No. The kit works on Pro, Max or Team accounts. Team makes the shared Project in the rollout plan easier, and on Team your chats are not used for training by default.

Can we stop the plan?

Yes. Turn off renewal anytime from your Gumroad receipt. Your team keeps the files you already downloaded.
