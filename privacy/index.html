Privacy

What this site collects, why, and what it never does.

The short version: I collect the least I can. No ad trackers, no selling data, no cookies for tracking. Your email is used only for what you signed up for.

Last updated 14 November 2026. This covers theusefulmedia.com, including The Useful Apps (/apps) and The Useful Library (/library). Wristkeeper and Textlore have their own privacy pages.

Who runs this

The Useful Media Co is a sole proprietorship run by Rajasekaran Ponnuchamy (Raja) in Chennai, India. Email raja@theusefultech.com about anything on this page.

What I collect, and why

  • Newsletter sign-ups. Your email address, and the page you signed up from. It goes to Beehiiv, the service that sends The Useful Tech and The Useful Life. Every email has a 1-click unsubscribe link.
  • Visitor counts. When a page loads, the site records the page, the site you came from, your screen width and campaign tags in the link. No cookie, no name, no full IP address. To keep out bots, a scrambled, shortened version of your IP address is used and cannot be turned back into it. Turn counting off on a device by opening any page with ?notrack=1 at the end of the address.
  • Things you send. App submissions, wrong-fact reports, comments and saves on The Useful Apps: what you typed, the app it is about and, if you gave one, your email. Used only to handle what you sent.
  • Saved apps and reading progress. Kept in your own browser. If you choose "Save my progress" on The Useful Library, your progress is stored with your email and a 4-digit PIN so you can open it on another device.
  • Payments. Guides are sold through Gumroad, and sponsorships and featured spots are paid through PayPal. They handle your card details. I see your name, email and what you bought.

Services that help run the site

  • Cloudflare: hosting, spam and bot checks (Turnstile) and the database for counts and submissions.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting.
  • Beehiiv: newsletters.
  • Gumroad and PayPal: payments.

Affiliate links

Some links to apps and books are affiliate links, including Amazon (as an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases), Setapp and app makers' own programs. If you buy through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what I recommend or how apps are ranked.

What I never do

  • Sell or rent your data.
  • Use advertising trackers or cross-site cookies.
  • Email you about things you did not sign up for.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any newsletter with the link in every email. To see, correct or delete anything I hold about you, email raja@theusefultech.com. I reply myself.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date above changes too.
