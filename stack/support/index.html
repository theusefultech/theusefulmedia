Common questions

Pro isn't showing after I paid

In Tinystack, open Settings, then Pro, and tap Restore at the bottom. Use the same Apple Account you paid with. Pro then turns on for your iPhone and Mac.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tinystack. Founding lifetime never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

My stacks aren't on my Mac

Both devices need the same Apple Account with iCloud on. Check that iCloud is on for Tinystack in Settings, then open the app on each device once. The Mac app comes with Pro.

Why don't I see AI picks?

On devices without Apple Intelligence, Tinystack plans with built-in rules. The screens look the same and every feature works. Devices with Apple Intelligence get picks from Apple's on-device model.

How does Together work?

Pro users invite a friend from the Together screen with a link. Your friend can join on the free plan.

Tinystack then shows times you are both free this week and a stack you both like. Only free and busy times and picked activity ids are shared, never event titles.

Film mode isn't saving my video

Film mode needs camera access and add-only Photos access. Turn both on in Settings, Tinystack. Keep the phone on a stand with the screen untouched; touching it ends the recording.

How do I add the widget or Control Center button?

Press and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Tinystack. For Control Center, open it, tap +, then Add a Control, and pick Start my stack or Urge rescue.

On iPhones with an Action button, choose Shortcut in Settings, Action Button, then Tinystack.

Can I add my own activities?

Yes, as many as you want, free. In Library tap + Your own, give it a name and pick its tags.
