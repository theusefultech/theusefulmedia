Questions

How did you pick these books?

Every book here has its own page in The Useful Library with a verdict, the ideas worth keeping and who should skip it. Each one is matched to the kind of person it suits best, not pulled from a bestseller list.

Where do the links go?

To the book on Amazon, in the right store for your country. If you buy, The Useful Library may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

Can I send a book by email?

Yes, many of these are on Kindle, and Amazon lets you send a Kindle book as a gift by email on the day you choose. Handy if the delivery date is too close.

What should I write inside?

Each pick comes with a short note you can copy and write inside the cover. A personal line is what makes a book feel like a gift.
