Questions

Does Claude send the emails?

No. Claude puts every draft in 1 document, client by client. A person checks each one and sends it.

What do I need to run it?

The Claude desktop app with Cowork on a paid plan, plus the Gmail or Outlook connector. Google Drive or SharePoint is optional for client files.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Each account manager runs it on their own clients. The Claude at Work Team Kit adds a rollout plan and 4 guides for teams of 5 to 50.
