In short

Wristkeeper is an iPhone app for watch collectors. Each morning it suggests 1 watch from your own collection, based on the weather, the occasion and how long each watch has waited, and says why in plain words. A press and hold logs the wear. Every wear adds to cost per wear, rotation and a monthly Wrist Report that turns into story slides and a short video to share. You can ask about your collection in plain words, answered on the iPhone. There is no account, no ads and no data collected: the collection stays on the iPhone and in the owner's own iCloud.
