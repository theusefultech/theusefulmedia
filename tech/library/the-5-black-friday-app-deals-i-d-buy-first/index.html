Vinaa: every AI model in 1 Mac app

Sponsor

If you pay for ChatGPT and keep a Claude tab open too, Vinaa puts both in 1 native Mac window, along with Gemini and local models from Ollama or LM Studio.

You can switch models in the middle of a chat, keep chats in projects and folders, and search every old conversation.

Bring your own API keys or connect the ChatGPT or Claude plan you already have.

The deal: $39 for 2 Macs instead of $70, or $30 for 1 Mac. Pay once. The price goes up to $99 on 1 Dec, so this runs until 30 Nov.

Nitro Photo: a Lightroom alternative you pay for once

Sponsor

Nitro PhotoPhoto editor and RAW manager for Mac, iPhone and iPadPrice and alternatives

Nitro Photo comes from the developer of RAW Power. It edits and organizes photos on Mac, iPhone and iPad, either inside your Photos library or in plain folders.

It reads RAW files from more cameras than Photos does, and adds AI masks, object removal and batch export with watermarks.

The deal: 50% off the lifetime license, and 1 purchase covers Mac, iPhone and iPad. Until 4 Dec.

Get Nitro Photo

Parallels Desktop: Windows apps on your Mac

Some things still only run on Windows: a work app, a game, an Excel feature the Mac version lacks.

Parallels DesktopRun Windows and Windows apps on your MacPrice and alternatives

Parallels sets up Windows in a few clicks and runs it in a window next to your Mac apps, with shared files and a shared clipboard. It rarely gets cheaper than this.

The deal: 50% off Standard and Pro with code PD50, for new 1-year plans. Until 30 Nov.

Dropzone: drag a file, drop it where it goes

Start dragging any file and Dropzone shows a grid of actions at the top of your screen. Drop the file on one to move it, AirDrop it, resize it or upload it.

DropzoneDrag files onto quick actions to move, share or uploadPrice and alternatives

It replaces the 2 Finder windows dance most of us do 20 times a day. The free version covers the basics.

The deal: 40% off Dropzone 5 Pro Lifetime with code BLACKFRIDAY26. Until 6 Dec.

Get Dropzone Pro

5of 5

Picmal: convert and shrink files without uploading them

Picmal turns a folder of HEIC photos into JPGs, shrinks a video small enough to email, cuts out photo backgrounds and merges PDFs. Hundreds of files at a time, all on your Mac, nothing sent to a website. It needs macOS 15 or later.

The deal: $14.50 for 1 Mac instead of $29, pay once. Until 30 Nov.

Get Picmal for $14.50
