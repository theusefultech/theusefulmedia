Using the app

Wristkeeper is for your own personal use. Its picks, insights and AI answers are there to help you choose. They can be wrong, so use your own judgement.

Your data

Everything you add stays on your iPhone. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you. If you delete the app, its data is deleted too. The privacy policy has the details.

Plans and payment

  • Wristkeeper offers monthly and yearly subscriptions, and a lifetime option you pay for once.
  • Prices are shown in the app, in your local currency, before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • A lifetime purchase never renews and has no further charges.

Free trial

A plan may start with a free trial. Its length is shown before you start. When the trial ends, the plan begins and you are charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

If you buy a plan during a trial, the rest of the trial ends.

Automatic renewal

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically for the same length of time. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

To stop this, turn off renewal at least 24 hours before the period ends. If a price ever changes, Apple will tell you first.
