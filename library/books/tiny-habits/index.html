The book in 60 seconds

BJ Fogg says behavior happens when motivation, ability and a prompt meet at the same moment. So make the habit tiny, anchor it right after something you already do, and celebrate the instant you do it. The good feeling is what wires it in, not repetition alone.

Your time

About 4 hours to read the parts that matter, instead of about 8 hours for the whole book.

Based on about 320 pages at 40 pages an hour, with skimmed parts counted at a third.

R

Raja's take

The celebration step will feel silly. Do it anyway. But the idea to steal is the anchor: do the new habit right after something you already do every day.

4 ideas from this book

1Ch. 1

B=MAP

A behavior happens when motivation, ability and a prompt meet. If it doesn't happen, find the 1 that's missing.

2Ch. 3

Scale it down

Floss 1 tooth. Do 2 push-ups. Make the habit small enough to do on a bad day.

3Ch. 4

After I..., I will...

Pick something you already do every day and put the new habit right after it.

4Ch. 5

Celebrate right away

Right after the habit, do something that makes you feel good, like a quiet yes or a fist pump.
