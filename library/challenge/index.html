Your 2027, month by month

4 areas, each 3 times. Every pick has chapter verdicts.

  1. Jan · HabitsStart the habit you keep restarting

    Pick 1 habit you've started and dropped at least twice. This month you rebuild it small enough that it survives February.

  2. Feb · FocusTake back 1 hour a day

    Find the hour your phone eats every day. Win it back for real work or real rest, and keep it for 30 days.

  3. Mar · MoneySee where your money actually goes

    Track every rupee, dollar or euro for 30 days. No budget yet. Just the truth, written down.

  4. Apr · MindsetStay steady when plans break

    Something will go wrong this month. Decide now how you'll answer it, then test that answer when it comes.

  5. May · HabitsBreak 1 habit you don't want

    Name the cue and the reward behind 1 habit you'd like gone. Keep both and swap the routine.

  6. Jun · FocusMake your phone boring

    Strip your phone back to the tools you chose on purpose. See what you reach for when the feed is gone.

  7. Jul · MoneyPut saving on autopilot

    Set up 1 automatic transfer on payday, so saving happens before you can spend it.

  8. Aug · MindsetWorry less about what you can't control

    Split every worry this month into 2 lists: yours to act on, and not yours. Act only on the first.

  9. Sep · FocusChoose what to neglect

    Write the 3 things you'll do badly or not at all this month, so the 1 thing that matters gets your best hours.

  10. Oct · HabitsKeep a habit alive on busy days

    Write the 2 minute version of your best habit. On the worst days of October, do only that.

  11. Nov · MoneyDecide what enough means

    Write your number for enough: the point where more money stops changing your life. Check 1 big purchase against it.

  12. Dec · MindsetReview the year you just read

    Go back through your 11 notes. Keep the 3 changes that lasted, and pick the problem for 2028.

Quick answers

Do I have to finish 12 books?

No. You read the chapters that matter in 1 book a month, about 3 to 4 hours, and test 1 idea. Most months you won't finish the whole book, and that's the point.

Which books do I read?

Each month gives you 3 picks from The Useful Library, each with read, skim or skip verdicts for every chapter. Any newer Library book in the same area counts too.

Can I start late?

Yes. Start on any month. The tracker keeps your place, and the Discord channel stays open all year.

Where do I get the books?

Anywhere: a library, Kindle, a used copy or the book links on each page. You don't need to buy all 12.

Is it only for one country?

No. The prompts work anywhere, and the money months use your own currency.

What happens in the Discord channel?

1 thread a month for that month's problem. Share what you tried, what worked and what didn't, with readers doing the same month as you.
