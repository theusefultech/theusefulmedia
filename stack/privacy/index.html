What we collect

Nothing. Tinystack is published by Rajasekaran Ponnuchamy (The Useful Media Co). It has no sign in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no server that receives your data, and we cannot see your activities, stacks, moods, goals or sessions. The sections below explain the Apple services the app uses for you.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your activities, stacks, sessions, mood check-ins and goals are kept in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so your iPhone and Mac stay in step. That data sits in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy and is not visible to us. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device you use.

Together

Together is only on when you invite a friend or accept an invite. It uses an iCloud share between your 2 Apple Accounts. Each side shares 2 things: free and busy times for the next 7 days, and the ids of the activities you picked. Event titles, places, people, moods and sessions are never shared. Either of you can end Together at any time, and the shared record is then deleted.

The planner

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Tinystack uses Apple's on-device model to build stacks, plan your day and write your weekly recap. That runs on your device. Other devices use rules built into the app. Either way, nothing you type or pick is sent to us or to any AI company.

Calendar

The day planner asks for calendar access the first time you open it. It reads your events on your device only to find free gaps. If you tap Add to Calendar, it writes that 1 event. Your events are never copied anywhere else.

Weather

If you allow location, the app uses your rough location to ask Apple Weather (WeatherKit) whether it is raining or hot, so it can pick indoor or outdoor activities. Apple handles that request. The app keeps no location history.

Film mode

Film mode asks for the camera the first time you choose it, and for add-only Photos access to save your time-lapse. The video is made on your device and saved to your Photos. It goes nowhere else unless you share it yourself. Tinystack cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Notifications and widgets

Your reminder, widgets, Live Activity and Siri shortcuts all run on your device. Nothing about them leaves it.

Purchases

Tinystack Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is active. It never sees your payment details. You manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting the app removes it from that device. On iPhone, remove the iCloud copy in Settings, then your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, Tinystack. On Mac, use System Settings, then your name, iCloud, Manage.

Children

Tinystack is not made for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact
