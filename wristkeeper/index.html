Wristkeeper is an iPhone app for people who own more than one watch. Every morning it picks one from your box, logs the wear in a tap and shows what each watch really costs you to wear.

A morning with Wristkeeper.

No setup rituals. It fits into the 2 minutes you already spend staring at your watch box.

7:30Today's pick is waiting

One watch, chosen for today.

It weighs the weather, what your day looks like and how long each watch has sat in the box, then tells you why in plain words. Dressing for dinner or the gym? Tap the occasion and it picks again.

7:31On your wrist

One tap logs the wear.

No forms and no typing. Forgot yesterday? Add any past day in a second. Your history fills itself in.

31stYour month, wrapped

A Wrist Report every month.

Your most worn watch, your rotation and your cost per wear, told as slides you can share, or as a video with Pro.

Every wear makes it a better buy.

Wristkeeper divides what you paid by how often you wear it. Pick a watch and see where the needle goes.
