    Questions

    Why would I need an app to use my phone less?

    You don't, forever. It gets you started and then gets out of the way. The hard part of putting the phone down is deciding what to do instead. Tinystack makes that decision in 1 tap.

    Is this just another focus timer?

    No. A timer counts down. Tinystack decides what to do with your time, from the things you enjoy, your calendar, the weather and how you felt last time.

    Do I have to film myself?

    No. Away mode is the default and nothing is recorded. Film mode is optional, about once a week, and the video stays on your phone unless you share it.

    What if my iPhone doesn't support Apple Intelligence?

    Every feature still works, using a simpler built-in planner. Apple's on-device AI makes the picks smarter on iPhone 15 Pro and newer.

    Is there an Android version?

    Not yet. The stack builder on this page works on any phone and computer, free.

    What happens to my data?

    It stays on your device and your own iCloud. No account, no server, nothing sold.
