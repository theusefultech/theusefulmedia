Questions

What is the most recommended money book?

Die With Zero by Bill Perkins. 2 of the 8 people on our bookshelves named it on video.

How is this list made?

We read what founders, scientists and authors say in their videos and podcasts, list every nonfiction book they recommend, and count how many different people named each one. Nobody pays to be on it. It updates every night.
