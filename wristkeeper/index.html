How it works

Wear every watch you own.

Most collectors wear the same 2 watches. Wristkeeper gets the rest back on your wrist in 3 steps.

01

Get a pick

Each morning, 1 watch from your box, chosen for the weather, the day and how long it has waited.

02

Log the wear

1 press and it's in the log. No forms, no typing. Missed a day? Add it later.

03

See the value

Every watch shows its wears and its real cost per wear, which drops each time you put it on.

And there's more

Small things collectors love.

Reads the weather

Rain on the way? It picks a diver on a bracelet, not your leather dress watch, and says why.

Your month, wrapped

The Wrist Report tells your month's story in slides you can share, or as a video with Pro.

Your whole box

Every watch with its wears, value and history. Ask it questions like "what haven't I worn lately?"

8 dial icons

Pro adds app icons drawn as watch dials, from Panda to Cola, plus 3 accent colours.

Your collection stays yours

Your watches, photos and wear log live on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud. We never see them.
