The book in 60 seconds

Vicki Robin and Joe Dominguez treat money as life energy: the hours you trade for it. Their 9 steps have you track every penny, price spending in hours of your life, ask whether each purchase brought fulfillment, and build savings until investment income covers your costs.

Your time

About 5.5 hours to read the parts that matter, instead of about 9.5 hours for the whole book.

Based on about 376 pages at 40 pages an hour, with skimmed parts counted at a third.

R

Raja's take

Start with step 2. Price your next big purchase in hours of your life. It's uncomfortable, and that's the point.

Quick answers

Is Your Money or Your Life worth reading?

Yes, but not all of it. Read 4 of its 9 steps: Step 2, Step 3, Step 4 and Step 8. They turn money into hours of your life, which changes how small buys feel.

Which parts of Your Money or Your Life should I read?

Read Step 2, Step 3, Step 4 and Step 8. Skim the parts marked skim for their 1 idea, and skip the rest on a first read.
