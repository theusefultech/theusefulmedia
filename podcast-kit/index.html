3 pricing page fixes that win back hesitant buyers

Your pricing page has 1 job: help a ready buyer pick a plan. Most pages make that harder than it needs to be. On episode 87 of The Lean Studio, Dana Reyes shared the 3 changes she makes first.

1. Cut to 3 plans

A 4th plan gives buyers a reason to stop and compare. 3 lets them choose in seconds.

2. Put 1 outcome line under each price

Not features. The result. "For teams shipping weekly" beats a list of 12 checkmarks.
