The questions every sponsor asks before booking

      
Answered here, so you can decide without waiting on my reply.

    

    

      
What does it cost?
A spot in a roundup is from $100, the top spot from $200, a newsletter feature from $150 and a dedicated review from $500. Full campaigns get a quote. The price you see when you book is the price you pay.

      
How many views does a sponsored roundup get?
Every post is different, so I don't promise a number. The Useful Tech has 78,756 followers, 3.39M views and a 50.2% read ratio, and my biggest app roundup has 156K views. Posts stay up for good, so Google keeps sending readers long after launch. All of it is public on my stats page.

      
Can I see stats for individual posts?
No. Per-post numbers include other sponsors' clicks and sales, so I keep them private for everyone, the same way I'll keep yours. You get my public stats page, live sample posts, and your own tracked link, which shows every click and sale the post sends you.

      
How do I see clicks and sales?
Medium doesn't show writers clicks on outbound links, so every sponsor gets their own tracked link. Send me a UTM or App Store campaign link and every click, install and sale shows up in your own analytics.

      
Where are your readers, and what do they read on?
53% are in the United States, followed by the UK, Australia, India and Canada. 89% read on a Mac or iPhone.

      
Is the post free to read?
Sponsored posts are member-only, like the rest of the blog, so Medium keeps recommending them to its paying readers. If you want yours free for everyone, say so when you book and it will be.

      
What's included, and can I add extras?
Every spot includes your own section, your link and a newsletter mention, and you approve the draft. Every placement is fixed as listed. Guarantees, content plans and multi-post deals come as a campaign package, so ask for one if you want more.

      
Is the post marked as sponsored?
Yes. Every sponsored post says so at the top, as Medium requires, and the newsletter feature is marked too. Readers trust the picks because the label is honest.

      
Does the post stay up?
For good. Sponsored posts never come down, and your link keeps working in every one.

      
What do you need from me?
A license or a TestFlight invite, your link, and anything I must get right. I install your app and use it before I write a word.

      
How fast can it go live?
You get the draft within 5 days of payment and your license. Once you approve it, it goes live on the next open day or a day you pick.

      
How does payment work?
You pay when you book, right on this page with PayPal in US dollars, and the draft starts as soon as it lands. Inside India, PayPal can't be used between Indian accounts, so you pay by UPI or bank transfer in rupees at that day's exchange rate. You still approve every line before the post goes live.

      
Can we get on a call?
No. Everything happens by email, so every detail stays in writing for both of us. I reply within 1 working day.
