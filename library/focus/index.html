Ideas

Essentialism

The 90 percent rule

Score each option out of 100 on what matters most to you. Anything below 90 counts as a no. If it isn't a clear yes, it's a clear no.

Deep Work

The shutdown ritual

End every workday the same way: check what's left, plan tomorrow, then say a set phrase that tells your brain work is done.

Deep Work

Take breaks from focus, not from distraction

Write down the next time you will check your phone. Until then, don't touch it, even in a queue.

Indistractable

Find the feeling before the distraction

Next time you switch away from work, write the feeling you had 1 second before. Then wait 10 minutes.

Digital Minimalism

Take 30 days off optional tech

List every app you open without a reason. Delete 1 of them from your phone today.
