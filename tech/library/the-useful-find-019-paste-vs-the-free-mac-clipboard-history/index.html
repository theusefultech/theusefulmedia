1 Mac App of the Year finalist that does what your free clipboard can't

Paste vs the clipboard history already built into macOS, and when each one is enough.

👋 HEY, IT'S RAJA

Apple named its 3 finalists for Mac App of the Year last week: Paste, Things 3 and Ulysses. Paste is the odd one out, because your Mac already comes with a free clipboard history.

So this week's find is Paste, and the real question behind it: when is the free one enough, and when is Paste worth paying for?

Today's ad partner makes a dictation app for Mac and iPhone.

🔍 The find: Paste

Paste is a clipboard manager for Mac, iPhone and iPad from Paste Team, a small studio that has been making it since 2015. It saves everything you copy, from text and links to images and files, and lets you search all of it later.

What your Mac already does for free

Since macOS 26 Tahoe, Spotlight keeps a clipboard history. It's off until you turn it on in System Settings, Spotlight, Results from Clipboard. After that, press Command Space, then Command 4, and you see what you copied recently.

It keeps items for 30 minutes, 8 hours or 7 days, and then they're gone. You can't pin anything to keep it, and the history stays on that 1 Mac. macOS 27 rebuilt Spotlight and left this part exactly as it was.

Where Paste goes further

  • History for as long as you want, all the way to unlimited.
  • Pinboards for the things you paste every week, like an email signature, a shipping address or a reply you send to every client.
  • Search that finds text inside old screenshots, not just text you copied.
  • Sync through your own iCloud, so a link you copy on your iPhone is waiting on your Mac.
  • The Paste Keyboard on iPhone, which puts your whole history inside any app.
  • A way to paste a list of items in order, 1 after another, instead of switching windows for each one.

Free or paid?

Stay with Spotlight if you copy and paste on 1 Mac and only need the last few hours back.

If you want a longer history on 1 Mac and don't want to pay, Maccy is free and open source.

Pay for Paste if you move things between your iPhone and Mac many times a day, or you keep snippets you reuse every week. That cross-device sync is most of what the money buys.

Tip: turn on Spotlight's clipboard history first and set it to 7 days. If you go looking for something older than a week more than twice, you need a real clipboard manager.
