Who should pick which

Pick Claude Motion if

  • Your company is on a Team or Enterprise plan and Motion is already on.
  • You want a quick animated chart or explainer from a report, inside the chat you are already in.
  • You want no install and no extra cost.
  • You are happy with a beta.

Pick Useful Studio if

  • You are on Pro or Max, where Motion is not available.
  • You want your logo, colours and fonts on every video without asking each time.
  • You need a vertical 9:16 cut next to the 16:9 one.
  • You want files on your Mac and a critic that checks the result.

Claude Motion is new and will change. This page reflects Anthropic's help article as of 22 Oct 2026. If Motion gains brand controls or reaches Pro, the gap narrows, and I will update this page.
