App Store preview video

Last updated 17 Nov 2026 · Apple's rules checked against Apple's own App Previews page on 10 Nov 2026

The short answer

An App Store preview is a video of up to 30 seconds, made from footage of your app, that autoplays with the sound off on your product page and in search results. Useful Studio Pro's /studio-appstore turns your own screen recordings into App Store and Google Play previews at every required size, from Claude Code on a Mac.

What Apple asks for

  • Up to 30 seconds each. You can add up to 3 previews for every language your app supports.
  • Footage from inside the app. No fingers tapping the screen and no over-the-shoulder shots of someone holding a phone.
  • Words on screen. Previews play muted by default, so short copy carries the story. Leave it up long enough to read.
  • No prices and no dates. Prices change by region, and "new for spring" ages the video.
  • Say when something costs extra. If a feature you show needs an in-app purchase, a subscription or a login, the preview has to say so, in the footage or on the end frame.
  • Device-specific files. Like screenshots, previews are made for each device size.

How to make one with Useful Studio Pro

  1. Record your app. Capture the 3 or 4 moments that sell it, as plain screen recordings.
  2. Run /studio-setup and /studio-brand once. Setup takes about 5 minutes with no admin password. Brand saves your app name, logo, colours, fonts and tone.
  3. Run /studio-appstore. Answer a few plain questions and point it at your recordings.
  4. Let the critic check it. Every film is scored on 7 things and the weakest is fixed until each score is 8 or more. It refuses text that moves while you read it.
  5. Upload the files. You get previews at every required size for the App Store and Google Play. The preview below took an 8 minute run.

Limits to know before you start

  • You bring the footage. It works from your own screen recordings. It does not generate AI images or footage of people.
  • Short only. Every Useful Studio video runs 15 to 90 seconds, and an App Store preview stops at Apple's 30.
  • Mac only. macOS 14 or later, with Claude Code on a paid Claude plan.

Questions people ask

How long can an App Store preview be?

Up to 30 seconds. Apple allows up to 3 previews for each language your app supports, and they autoplay with the sound off on your product page and in search results.

Can I put graphics or text in an App Store preview?

Yes. Apple allows graphics such as touch hotspots, and recommends copy because previews play muted. The footage itself must show only what happens inside your app, with no hands or devices filmed from outside.
