Get the book

The book in 60 seconds

Small habits compound into big results. James Clear argues you change behavior by fixing your systems, not your goals, and by building an identity ("I am a reader") that each habit proves. His 4 laws: make a habit obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying, and flip them to break a bad one.

Your time

About 3.5 hours to read the parts that matter, instead of about 8 hours for the whole book.

Based on about 322 pages at 40 pages an hour, with skimmed parts counted at a third.

R

Raja's take

Read the part on environment first. I keep books in every room, picked for that room. I read more now and I never had to try harder. That's the whole book in 1 move.

Never miss twice

Where books disagree

Atomic Habits says shrink a new habit to 2 minutes. Getting Things Done says do any task under 2 minutes right now. Same name, different rule.

Is Atomic Habits worth reading?

Yes, but not all of it. Read 6 of its 20 chapters: ch. 1, ch. 2, ch. 3, ch. 5, ch. 13 and ch. 16. 6 chapters carry the whole system; the rest repeats it with stories.

Which parts of Atomic Habits should I read?

Read ch. 1, ch. 2, ch. 3, ch. 5, ch. 13 and ch. 16. Skim the parts marked skim for their 1 idea, and skip the rest on a first read.
