What is Rosewood Music Box?

A 3D wind-up music box for iPhone. Wind the brass key, open the lid and a porcelain ballerina rises and dances. It plays 6 public domain pieces: Gymnopédie No. 1, Für Elise, Swan Lake, Brahms' Lullaby, Greensleeves and Twinkle Twinkle.

How does it work on iPhone Duo?

The phone is the box. Closed, the outer screen is the lid: drag around the key to wind it. Open the phone and the top half becomes the lid with its mirror, while the bottom half shows the inside of the box. Lay it flat to open the Cylinder Cabinet and pick a tune. Close the phone and the music stops.

Does it work on other iPhones?

Yes. Wind the key, then flick the lid up to open the box. Tap the nameplate to open the Cylinder Cabinet and change the tune.

Why did the music slow down?

Like a real music box, the spring runs down. The tune slows near the end and stops. Wind the key again to keep it playing.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment, so Apple handles refunds too. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data does it collect?

None. No account, no analytics, no ads, and the app never goes online. The details are in the privacy policy.
