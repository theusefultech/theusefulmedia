Your Mac, on every screen you own

A brand-new app that streams your Mac to iPad, iPhone, even Vision Pro

👋 HEY, IT'S RAJA

Welcome to the first Monday find. Every week in this slot, I'll bring you one app that most people haven't heard of yet — tested, explained, and worth your attention. Today's pick launched last month, is still flying under the radar, and solves a problem I suspect a lot of you have: a powerful Mac sitting at a desk, and a much more portable device in your bag.

🔍 This week's find: Mirage

Your Mac, streamed to your iPad, iPhone, or Vision Pro — at Retina quality, with almost no lag.

What it does

Mirage streams your Mac's screen to your iPhone, iPad, Vision Pro, or even another Mac, with very low latency and Retina-quality output. On iPad, it fully supports your Magic Keyboard and Apple Pencil — so it genuinely feels like using your Mac, not remote-controlling it through a fuzzy window.

Why it's useful

  • Window-level streaming. You don't have to mirror the whole desktop — Mirage can stream a single app window. Keep iPadOS for everything else and pull in just the one Mac app you need.
  • Remote access via Tailscale. Put Tailscale (or a similar VPN) on both devices, and you can reach your home Mac from anywhere — coffee shop, airport, in-laws' place.
  • A generous free tier. Local network streaming is free; Mirage Pro adds remote access, multi-window support, better audio, and the highest streaming quality.

Who it's for

Anyone running a desktop-first setup — a Mac mini or Mac Studio at home with an iPad as the portable machine. With Apple's recent Mac price increases, this setup just got even more sensible: one powerful desktop, streamed to every screen you already own.

📱 Platform: Mac + iPhone, iPad, Vision Pro (iOS 26 and later)

💰 Price: Free for local streaming · Mirage Pro (subscription or lifetime) for remote access and top quality

🔁 Alternative to: Sidecar, Luna Display, Jump Desktop
