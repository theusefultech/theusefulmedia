Wristkeeper tells you which watch to wear every morning.

It is a collection and wear log for people who own more watches than wrists. Each morning it picks one for you based on the weather, the occasion, and what you wore last.

Four things Wristkeeper does for your collection.

Picks today's watch

A morning suggestion that weighs the weather, where you're going, and what you wore this week.

Logs every wear

One tap records what's on your wrist, and a month dial shows your wear history at a glance.

Holds your collection

Photos, brands, dial colours, and straps, with filters to see your collection any way you like.

Shows the patterns

Most and least worn, cost per wear, and the watches that haven't left the box this month.

Your collection never leaves your iPhone.

  • No accountsOpen the app and start adding watches.
  • No analyticsNothing tracks how you use it.
  • No serversYour photos and wear history stay on your device.
