The 2 minute rule

Shrink any new habit to a version that takes 2 minutes. "Read 30 pages" becomes "read 1 page". Starting is the hard part, so make starting tiny.

Open the idea

Try it today · 3 steps

1. Pick 1 habit you keep putting off.

2. Cut it to its first 2 minutes. Running becomes putting on your shoes.

3. Do only that today. Stop there, even if you want more.
