The 4 lessons

Each lesson has the steps written out, a screen recording of me doing them, and the files to copy. You build yours that week.

Mon 11 Jan

Set up your Mac for Claude

The Claude desktop app, scheduled tasks and Claude Code, and what each one is for. You connect your email, calendar and files, then write the rules file that makes Claude write like you. I show it with Gmail and Google Calendar.

This week you set Claude up to draft replies to your own inbox, in your voice.

  • A lesson reaches your inbox every Monday for 4 weeks.

  • Send questions about your own setup to raja@theusefultech.com on any day.
  • Every Thursday the whole group gets that week's questions with my answers.
  • There are no calls and no video meetings.

Who's teaching

I'm Raja. I run The Useful Tech, the #1 Apple blog on Medium, with 78K Medium followers and 500+ articles. It's a 1-person company, and I'm not a developer.

Claude built my iPhone app, which Apple approved on 13 November 2026, and my Mac app, on sale since October 2026. It built this site too. Scheduled tasks draft my newsletters and post my daily reel, and another task checks them 2 times a day.

Claude does

  • Drafts the replies and reports
  • Builds the small tools
  • Runs every job on its schedule
  • Checks its own runs every day

I do

  • Pick which jobs to hand off
  • Read 1 check-in email a day
  • OK every email about money

It fits if

  • You run your own work as a freelancer, creator, consultant or solo founder
  • You pay for Claude and mostly use it as a chat window
  • You have a list of jobs you redo every week

Skip it if

  • You're a developer who already lives in Claude Code
  • You want live calls or video. This one is written, with silent screen recordings

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. In lesson 3 you talk to Claude Code in plain English, and it writes the code.

Which Claude plan do I need?

Any paid plan. Pro includes scheduled tasks and Claude Code, so it is enough to follow along. Lesson 4 shows how much each kind of job uses, so you know if your plan covers what you want to run.

Are there any calls?

No. Everything arrives by email: the written lessons, the screen recordings and the answers to your questions.

What if I fall behind?

Each lesson stays in your inbox, so build it when you have time. Questions are open until Sunday 14 February.

Does my Mac have to stay on?

Not for scheduled tasks. They can run in the cloud, so they run with your Mac off. A tool that works on files on your Mac runs when the Mac is on.

What happens if fewer than 5 people join?

The cohort doesn't run. Everyone gets a full refund on Friday 11 December, with nothing to request.

Can I get a refund after 11 December?

No. Once 5 people join, the cohort runs and all sales are final. The lessons are yours to keep.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. It is an independent cohort by The Useful Media Co. It is not made or endorsed by Anthropic.
