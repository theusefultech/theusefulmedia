Questions

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. It's a Mac app only.

Will it get in the way of my work?

No. Every click goes straight through the weather to what's under it. It pauses by itself when a full-screen app or video is open, and you can pause it for an hour from the menu bar.

Will it slow my Mac down?

It draws on the graphics chip, stops when the screen sleeps and runs at half speed on battery.

Does it need any permissions?

None for the weather and effects. Skypane only reads where your windows are, never what's in them. Real Sky asks for your location once, or you can type a city instead.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it, with every update.
