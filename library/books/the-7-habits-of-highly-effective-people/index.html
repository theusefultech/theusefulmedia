The book in 60 seconds

Stephen Covey sets out 7 habits in 3 stages. Habits 1 to 3 are about running your own life: be proactive, begin with the end in mind, put first things first.

Habits 4 to 6 are about working with others. Habit 7 is about renewing yourself so the rest keep working.

Your time

About 4.5 hours to read the parts that matter, instead of about 9 hours for the whole book.

Based on about 358 pages at 40 pages an hour, with skimmed parts counted at a third.

R

Raja's take

Don't try to read it in a week. Read Habit 3 first, draw the grid, and plan 1 week with it. Come back for Habit 5 the next time a conversation goes wrong.

4 ideas from this book

1Habit 1

Circle of influence

Spend your energy on what you can change, not on everything you worry about.

2Habit 2

Begin with the end in mind

Write down what you want people to say about you, and let it guide your choices.

3Habit 3

Quadrant II

Plan important but not urgent work first, every week, before urgent things fill the week.

4Habit 5

Listen to understand

Repeat back what the other person means before you give your view.
