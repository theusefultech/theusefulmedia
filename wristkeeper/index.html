How it works

Pick. Wear. See.

  1. 1

    Pick

    Every morning, a watch from your box that fits the weather and your day. Dressing for something? Tap Work, Dinner, Gym or Travel.

  2. 2

    Wear

    Hold once and the wear is logged. Forgot yesterday? Add any past day in a second. Your history fills itself in.

  3. 3

    See

    Cost per wear for every watch, your most and least worn, and a Wrist Report when the month ends.

Cost per wear

What does your watch really cost?

The price tag is only half the story. Divide it by the days it was on your wrist. Every wear makes a watch better value, and Wristkeeper keeps that number for each one.

Questions, answered.

Where does my collection data live?

In your own iCloud. There is no Wristkeeper account, no sign-up and no ads.

What's free?

Up to 3 watches, forever, with the daily pick, the wear log, cost per wear and the Wrist Report. Pro unlocks the rest of your box.

Is it subscription only?

No. Pro is $24.99 a year with 14 days free, or $3.99 a month. Lifetime is $59.99, once.

How do I join the beta?

Tap Join the free beta on your iPhone. It opens TestFlight, Apple's app for testing apps before launch. Pro is free while you test.

Can I import my list from another app?

Not yet. The sample watches get you started, and adding your own takes a photo, a name and a price.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

Your watches and history stay. The Pro extras pause until you come back.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Wristkeeper is iPhone only for now. Leave your email below and you'll hear if that changes.

When does it launch?

On the App Store on 17 November 2026. The beta is open now.
