Three rules every post, product, and partnership has to follow.

#1

Useful the same day

If you can't use it today, it doesn't get published. A setting, an app, a habit, or a system you can copy.

#2

Sponsors are labelled

Paid placements are marked clearly, so you always know what's an ad and what's my pick.

#3

I sell what I use

Every guide and template starts as a system running on my own Mac before it goes on Gumroad.
