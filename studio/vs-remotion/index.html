Useful Studio vs Remotion

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · Facts checked against each project's own pages on 22 Oct 2026

The short answer

Remotion is a React framework for making video with code, free for individuals and teams of up to 3 people. Useful Studio is a $79 Claude Code skill pack for Mac that makes branded short videos from 1 typed line, with no React to write.

Who should pick which

Pick Remotion if

  • You are a developer and want video as code that you own and can version.
  • You need personalised or data-driven videos, or a player inside your app.
  • You need videos longer than 90 seconds.
  • You are not on a Mac.

Pick Useful Studio if

  • You want a branded 25 to 90 second video today without writing React.
  • You want a saved brand, templates and a built-in critic.
  • You are a creator or app maker, not a video developer.
  • You are on a Mac with a paid Claude plan.

Cost check. Remotion is free for a solo maker, so the real price is your time learning it. If you are a solo developer who enjoys React, Remotion costs $0 and gives you more control than Useful Studio ever will. If a company of 4 or more makes videos with it, the Creators license costs $25 per seat per month, which is $300 a year per person.
