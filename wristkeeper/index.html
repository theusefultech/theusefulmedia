The right watch, every morning.

Wristkeeper is an iPhone app for watch collectors. It picks today's watch from the ones you own, logs each wear with 1 hold and shows what every watch really costs you to wear.

Free your safe queens.

Each morning Wristkeeper suggests 1 watch from your own box. It weighs the weather, the occasion and how long each watch has waited, then tells you why in plain words.

Hold to log the wear.

Press and hold, and the wear is logged. No forms, no typing. The month ring fills as you go, so your rotation shows at a glance.

Your real cost per wear.

Every wear makes a watch better value. Wristkeeper divides what you paid by how often you wear it, for each watch and for the whole collection.

$5.00 a wear

Picks that read the weather.

Rain or heat, the daily pick knows what to wear today. On a wet Thursday it picks a 200m diver on a bracelet, and says why.

Your month, on the wrist.

At the end of each month the Wrist Report tells the story: your most worn watch, your rotation, your cost per wear and how the month compares with the last. Share it as slides, or as a video with Pro.

8 dial icons, your colours.

Pro adds 8 app icons drawn as watch dials, from Black to Night, with Cola and Panda in between, plus 3 accent themes for the app.

Your collection stays yours.

Your watches, photos and wear log live on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud. We never see them.
