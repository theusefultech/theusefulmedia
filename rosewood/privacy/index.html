Published by Rajasekaran Ponnuchamy. Rosewood Music Box is a 3D wind-up music box for iPhone. It has no accounts, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It never connects to the internet.

What we collect

Nothing. The app sends no data to us or to anyone else. There is no server behind it.

What stays on your iPhone

The music, the 3D box and every sound are made on your iPhone. The app reads your iPhone's hinge angle and motion sensors only to open the lid and move the light, and that reading never leaves the device.

Purchase

Rosewood Music Box is a paid app sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If any of the above ever changes, this policy will be updated before the app behaves differently.
