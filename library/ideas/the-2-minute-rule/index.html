The 2 minute rule

Same name, 2 different rules. Both work. They fix different problems.

Shrink the habit

When you start a new habit, it should take less than 2 minutes. "Do 30 minutes of yoga" becomes "take out my yoga mat". Master showing up first. Grow it later.

James Clear · chapter 13 is the 1 to read

Fixes: habits you never start.

GTD

Just do it now

If a task takes less than 2 minutes, do it the moment you notice it. Writing it down and tracking it would take longer than doing it.

David Allen, Getting Things Done

Fixes: small tasks that pile up.

Try it today · tap each step when done
