The phone is the box

Close it, open it or lay it flat.

Every way you hold your iPhone Duo does something a real music box would do.

Closed

Wind the brass key

The outer screen is the lid. Drag on it and every turn clicks like a real spring.

Open

Lift the lid

The top half becomes the lid and its mirror. The bottom half is the box, where she rises and dances.

Flat

Pick a cylinder

Lay the phone flat and the Cylinder Cabinet opens. Tap one to change the tune.

The lid

She dances in the lid's mirror.

The mirror is a true reflection, so it shifts as you fold the phone, just like tilting a real lid.

Inside

Watch every pin pluck the comb.

  • Built in 3D, down to the last steel tooth.
  • The music slows as the spring runs down.
  • Opening the lid lets the sound out. Closing it muffles it.
  • The light moves as you tilt your phone.
  • Tap the dancer for an extra twirl.
