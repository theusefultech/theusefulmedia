Ideas

Atomic Habits vs Getting Things Done

The 2 minute rule

2 books, 2 different rules. Shrink a new habit, or do small tasks now.

FULL PAGEAtomic Habits, ch. 5

Habit stacking

Tie a new habit to one you already do every day: after I pour my morning coffee, I will write 1 line in my journal.

Atomic Habits, ch. 16

Never miss twice

Missing a day happens. Missing 2 in a row is how a habit quietly dies. Treat the day after a miss as the one that counts.

The Power of Habit

Keep the cue, swap the routine

You can't delete a bad habit easily, but you can change what you do when the urge hits, as long as you still get a reward.

Tiny Habits

Make it so small you can't fail

Cut your habit to a version that takes under 30 seconds. Do only that version this week.

Atomic Habits

Never miss twice

Decide now what your habit looks like on a bad day. If you miss today, put that version first on tomorrow's list.

The Power of Habit

Plan the hard moment before it comes

Write the moment you usually give up, like coming home tired. Next to it, write exactly what you will do in that moment instead.
