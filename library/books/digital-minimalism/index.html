The book in 60 seconds

Cal Newport argues for using a few tools on purpose instead of every app by default. The core is a 30-day digital declutter, followed by rebuilding your tech use around what you value. He fills the freed time with solitude, real conversation and demanding leisure.

Your time

About 4.5 hours to read the parts that matter, instead of about 7.5 hours for the whole book.

Based on about 295 pages at 40 pages an hour, with skimmed parts counted at a third.

R

Raja's take

I tried a dopamine detox once. It didn't stick. What stuck was smaller: piano breaks instead of phone breaks, and walks without earbuds. The chapter on solitude is the one to keep.
