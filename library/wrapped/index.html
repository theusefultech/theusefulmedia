    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. No sign-up, no email needed to make your story.

    Where does my list go?

    Nowhere. It stays in this browser on this device. Only the book searches go to Open Library to find covers and page counts. Your share link carries the list inside the link itself.

    How do I import from Goodreads?

    On the Goodreads website, open My Books, then Import and export, then Export Library. Download the file and pick it here. We keep the books on your Read shelf with a 2026 date.

    How do I import from StoryGraph?

    On the StoryGraph website, open your profile menu, then Manage Account, then Export StoryGraph Library. Download the file from the email and pick it here.

    How is my reader type picked?

    By simple rules on your list: how many books, how long they are, how old they are and what they're about. Nothing is guessed by AI.
