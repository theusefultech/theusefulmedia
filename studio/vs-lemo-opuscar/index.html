Who should pick which

Pick lemo-opuscar if

  • You want cinematic, art-directed short films with a distinct look.
  • You like to tinker and an afternoon per film is fine.
  • You want 59 styles to choose from and $0 to spend.
  • You want to read and change every style prompt yourself.

Pick Useful Studio if

  • You ship videos every month and want them to look like the same company made them.
  • You need 16:9 and 9:16 cuts, captions and a voiceover without extra steps.
  • You make launch films for an app, and you want App Store previews (Pro).
  • You want someone to email when setup breaks.

The two do not compete for the same job as much as the price gap suggests. lemo-opuscar is a gallery of looks you direct yourself. Useful Studio is a set of commands that turn 1 line about your product into a finished file. Try lemo-opuscar first if you are unsure. It is free and it is good.
