Why is the pilot free?

Because you've never heard of me. 30 days of real meetings proves more than anything I could write here. If the meetings come, you'll want to keep going. If they don't, you owe nothing.

Do I pay you anything during the pilot?

You pay for your own sending setup, about $53 a month, and you keep it. That's it. No card on file with us, no contract, no setup fee.

How many meetings will I get?

I won't promise a number I can't see yet. It depends on your area, your services and how fast you follow up. You'll see every send, reply and meeting each Friday, and the full count in the report before you decide anything.

What counts as a meeting?

A decision-maker at a business in your size range and area who agreed to a call about IT and booked it on your calendar. People who only asked a question go in the report as replies, not meetings.

Will this hurt my main domain?

No. Nothing is sent from your company domain. The new domains look like yours and send visitors to your site, so a bad day never touches your real email.

Do you need my passwords?

No. You buy the domains and inboxes yourself and connect them to the sending tool with a Google sign-in link. We never see or store a password.

Can I approve the emails first?

Yes. You see all 3 emails before anything goes out, and you can change any line. You can also tell us anything you never want said.

Who writes the emails and replies?

I do, with AI tools for research and drafts. Every email and every reply is checked before it goes out. Each one uses facts about that business, so none of them read like a template.

Will you contact my current clients?

No. Send us your client list and any business you never want contacted. We remove them before the first send.

Will you work with another MSP in my area?

Not during your pilot or while you keep going. 1 MSP per area, so you never compete with your own outreach.

Is cold email legal?

In the US, emailing businesses is allowed under CAN-SPAM when the email is honest, says who it's from, includes a postal address and lets people opt out. Every email does all 3, and opt-outs are removed the same day.

Who owns the list and the replies?

You do. The list, every reply and every meeting are yours to keep, whether you continue or stop.

What happens after 30 days?

You get the report and decide. Keep going at $1,200 a month, locked for 6 months, or stop with no bill. If you stop, you keep the domains, inboxes, list and meetings.
